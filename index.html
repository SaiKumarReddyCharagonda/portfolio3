Academic Portfolio · Artifact 02

AI-Powered Customer Churn Prediction Using Machine Learning

Applying predictive modeling to a customer retention challenge

1. Introduction

Artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) help organizations analyze data and make informed decisions. Customer churn occurs when customers stop using a company's products or services. Predicting churn can help businesses identify customers who may leave, improve customer retention, and maintain long-term relationships.

2. Description

This project explores how machine learning can identify customers who may discontinue a service. The proposed model analyzes customer engagement, purchase history, subscription activity, and support interactions to identify patterns associated with churn. These predictions can help businesses develop targeted retention strategies and make better-informed customer relationship decisions.

3. Objective

The objective is to demonstrate how machine learning can address customer retention challenges. The project explores customer data analysis, predictive modeling, model evaluation, and responsible AI practices while explaining technical concepts in a way that nontechnical audiences can understand.

4. Process
  1. Define the problem: Treat customer churn prediction as a supervised machine learning classification problem.
  2. Collect and inspect data: Use historical customer information and review the available features and churn target variable.
  3. Prepare the data: Check for missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent entries, and data types. Apply suitable preprocessing and encoding where needed.
  4. Split the dataset: Divide the prepared data into training and testing sets so model performance can be evaluated on unseen records.
  5. Build the model: Use logistic regression as a classification approach to estimate the likelihood that a customer will churn.
  6. Evaluate performance: Review accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and the confusion matrix to understand the model's strengths and limitations.
  7. Interpret and apply findings: Use validated results to help prioritize customer outreach and service improvements, with human oversight and ongoing model monitoring.
5. Tools and Technologies Used
PythonPandasScikit-learnMatplotlibSeabornJupyter NotebookGoogle ColabSalesforce CRM (future integration)

Python supports data analysis and model development. Pandas can be used to organize and clean datasets, while Scikit-learn provides machine learning algorithms and evaluation methods. Matplotlib and Seaborn support data visualization. Jupyter Notebook or Google Colab can be used to develop and document the project. Salesforce CRM could support a future integration of validated predictions.

Implementation note: List tools as used only when they were actually used in the project. Salesforce CRM is identified as a possible future integration, not as a completed implementation.
6. Value Proposition

The proposed solution can help businesses identify potential churn risks and prioritize customer retention efforts. By identifying patterns in customer behavior, the model may support better resource allocation, improved customer engagement, and data-driven decisions. The usefulness of the predictions depends on data quality, model performance, and appropriate business interpretation.

7. Unique Value

This artifact connects machine learning concepts with a practical business challenge. In addition to predictive analytics, the project emphasizes responsible AI practices, including data privacy, fairness, transparency, and human judgment. These considerations are important because churn predictions may influence how organizations communicate with and make decisions about customers.

8. Relevance

Customer churn prediction is relevant to telecommunications, banking, retail, and subscription-based businesses. Organizations in these industries can use predictive insights to better understand customer behavior and plan retention activities. This project demonstrates my developing understanding of how AI can support business decisions and address organizational problems.

9. Expected Learning Outcome

Through this project, I aim to strengthen my ability to prepare real-world data, build and evaluate a classification model, interpret performance measures, and explain how predictive analytics can support responsible business decisions.

Academic Portfolio · Artifact 03

Professor Predict — Understanding Large Language Models

Exploring next-token prediction, model training, hallucinations, and responsible AI use

1. Introduction

This artifact documents my participation in an educational activity designed to explain how large language models (LLMs) generate responses, learn language patterns, and sometimes produce inaccurate information. Sentence predictions, training-data examples, and practical task classifications were used to discuss the strengths and limitations of AI during the activity.

2. Description

The activity examined next-token prediction, pretraining, post-training, training-data availability, hallucinations, and responsible AI use. One example involved a fictional nation called Velmora. Recognizing that the country was fictional helped demonstrate why a plausible question does not necessarily have a factual answer.

The activity also compared suitable AI tasks, such as drafting and brainstorming, with tasks requiring additional verification, including current financial information, precise calculations, and personalized legal guidance.

3. Objective

The goal was to gain insight into the inner workings of LLMs and critically reflect on the contexts in which they can be used appropriately. The activity also aimed to strengthen my ability to distinguish unsupported claims from verified factual knowledge and recognize the difference when evaluating AI-generated language.

4. Process

I followed the educational activity by analyzing familiar sentence patterns, discussing a question about the fictional country, and examining the concepts of pretraining, post-training, and general categories of training data. I then classified different tasks according to whether an LLM could reasonably assist without additional tools or whether independent verification was necessary.

I discussed the reasons for these classifications and reflected on the importance of critical thinking, source checking, and human judgment when working with information generated by artificial intelligence.

5. Tools and Technologies Used
ChatGPT / LLMPretraining conceptsPost-training conceptsCritical evaluationFact-checkingGitHubMarkdown
  • ChatGPT or another commercial AI tool for the educational interaction.
  • Large language model concepts, including pretraining and post-training.
  • Critical evaluation and fact-checking principles.
  • GitHub and Markdown for documenting and presenting the portfolio artifact.
6. Value Proposition

This activity strengthened my ability to explain basic AI concepts, assess the credibility of generated answers, and identify when additional evidence or tools are required. These skills are valuable in business, research, and technology-related decisions and applications of AI.

7. Unique Value

The activity links technical AI concepts to real-world examples and scenarios that are understandable to a nontechnical audience. It highlights that a fluent response may appear correct even when it is inaccurate, making context, verification, and human judgment essential to effective AI use.

8. Relevance

Understanding LLM capabilities and limitations is important when organizations use AI for communication, research, customer service, automation, and decision support. This artifact demonstrates foundational AI literacy and responsible evaluation of AI-generated content.

9. Learning Reflection

The activity helped me recognize that LLM responses are generated through learned language patterns and are not automatically verified facts. I learned to consider the task, check important claims against reliable sources, and use human judgment when the consequences of an inaccurate answer could be significant.
